## Before/after pairs

The before/after sliders are **illustrative example pairs**: the two photos

severe damage vs. typical staining, wet floor vs. drying in progress).
They are stock photos, not photographs of BayouDry jobs, and are labeled
as such wherever they appear. Never present them as "our work."
